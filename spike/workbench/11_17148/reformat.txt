- To relieve tight calf muscles, you can try several methods.
- Firstly, if tightness or cramping occurs during a run, find a curb and rest the forefoot of the cramping leg on it, then slowly lower your heel off the curb and hold for 30 seconds.
- If the cramping intensifies, gently massage the area.
- After running, consider seeing a sports massage therapist or physical therapist for a deep tissue massage.
- Alternatively, self-massage at home using a massage ball or foam roller can be beneficial.
- Performing calf raises can also help, by standing with your hands holding the back of a chair or table, slowly raise your heels off the ground, hold for a few seconds, relax and repeat.
- Lastly, calf stretches can be performed, hold each stretch for 20 seconds or more, doing three to five sets per leg, and remember to breathe slowly in through your nose and out through your mouth during each stretch.
