- Begin by trimming fat from your beef tenderloin steaks, which should be cut 3/4 inch to 1 inch thick.
- Season as desired.
- Optionally, you can brown the tenderloin first in a large skillet with olive oil over medium-high heat to caramelize the outside and lock in the juices.
- After, place the steak in a pan and sear for 1 minute.
- Then, place the entire pan in the oven for about 5 minutes.
- Remove the steaks from the oven and place them to the side in a warm location on a plate.
- They will continue to cook on their own, bringing them to a nice medium-rare while allowing the muscle to relax and the juices to settle within the meat.
