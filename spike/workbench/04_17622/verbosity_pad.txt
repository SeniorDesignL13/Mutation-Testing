The response is structured around the question's wording and the context in which it appears. To cook a Cuban Boston butt pork roast in the oven, you first need to prepare a seasoning mix. Puree garlic, oregano, olive oil, limes, and salt in a blender or food processor. Place the pork butt roast in a roasting pan, score the fat on top of the pork, and rub the garlic mixture all over it. Cover and refrigerate the meat overnight or up to 24 hours, making sure to flip the roast occasionally and redistribute the rub. Preheat the oven to 250 degrees and remove the meat from the refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking. Place the pan in the oven, fat side up, and add 1 cup of water to the pan. Roast the meat uncovered, occasionally basting it with juices and turning the pan for even cooking. Cook the roast for 6 to 8 hours, until the meat easily shreds. After removing the roast from the oven, let it rest for 20 minutes, then pull the roast into shreds with a fork, leaving the meat in the pan juices. It's now ready to serve.
