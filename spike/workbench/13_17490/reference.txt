ORIGINAL ANSWER
To tie a water balloon, first fill the balloon and loop it over the top. While holding the neck of the balloon, ensure it is under a tool, then spin the tool counter-clockwise in a full circle. You can also use a gadget called Tie Not, which can be attached to a water hose for a quick twirl and tie or used independently as a tying assistant. Alternatively, stretch the opening of the balloon's lip around the faucet and hold it in place. Ensure the balloon doesn’t slip off as it begins to take on water. If the balloon's neck is slippery, gently and rapidly tug on it a few times to get it good and loose for tying, aiming for 3-6 inches of give when you stretch out the neck.
