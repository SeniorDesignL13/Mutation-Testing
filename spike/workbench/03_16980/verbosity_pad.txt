To name your Linksys router, first connect an Ethernet cable from any of the numbered Ethernet ports on your Linksys router to the Ethernet port on your computer. Make sure the router is turned on. Then open your browser and enter 192.168.1.1 in the top address field, which is the default IP address for most Linksys routers (if you changed your router's IP address, enter that instead). Next, enter 'admin' as the login username and password (for some Linksys routers, the username may be left blank).
