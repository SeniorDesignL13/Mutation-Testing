ORIGINAL ANSWER
The factory system affected both labor organization and social structures. The development of large machines required a new way of organizing labor, which resulted in many machines being installed together in factories and workers having long, regular hours from dawn to dusk, six days a week. The rise of factories also led to a shift from a more communal society with informal social structures to a more rationalized society with more formal structures. Work and home became separate, and informal social pressures could no longer maintain order among large groups of people who didn't know each other well.
