- The chances of getting HIV after a single encounter vary depending on the type of sexual activity.
- For unprotected receptive anal sex, the risk is estimated to be 1.4%.
- For an HIV-negative woman having unprotected receptive vaginal sex, the risk could be closer to 1%.
- A study of heterosexual couples in Africa estimated the chance of catching the AIDS virus from a single sexual encounter with an infected person to be one in 588.
