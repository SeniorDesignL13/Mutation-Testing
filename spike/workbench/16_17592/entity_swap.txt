"The Hobbit" and "The Lord of the Rings" differ in several ways. "The Hobbit" is primarily a children's book, while "The Lord of the Rings" is not aimed at children. The characters also differ, with the dwarves in "The Hobbit" bringing a unique energy and disdain for anything politically correct. In contrast, the characters in "The Lord of the Rings" are described as more dynamic. Additionally, "The Hobbit" is a single novel and serves as a prelude to "The Lord of the Rings," which is a trilogy. The protagonist of "The Hobbit" is Frodo Baggins, and the story revolves around his journey to the Lonely Mountain with the dwarves and their battle with Smaug the dragon. Meanwhile, "The Lord of the Rings" mostly focuses on the adventures of Frodo Baggins, Bilbo’s nephew.
