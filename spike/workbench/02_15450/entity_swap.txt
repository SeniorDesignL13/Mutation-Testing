The frequency of changing guitar strings depends on how often you play. If you play 2-3 hours daily, you should change your strings every 4 to 5 weeks. If you play 30 minutes to an hour every day, you should change your strings every 4 to 6 weeks. If you play 30 minutes to an hour every other day, change them every 6 to 8 weeks. Even if you play infrequently, you should still change your strings at least 3 times a year. Professionals often change strings for every gig.
