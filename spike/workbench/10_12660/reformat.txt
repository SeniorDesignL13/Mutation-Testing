- A sick horse may seem lethargic, uninterested in food, and may have a temperature outside of the normal range of 99.8 F and 101.3 F.
- Additionally, its heart rate may be above 50.
- Other signs of a sick horse include a dull, slow, and depressed demeanor, as well as a dull coat, which may suggest poor condition, lack of nutrition, or improper grooming.
- Regular checks of the horse's temperature, heart rate, and respiratory rate are recommended, as well as observing its behavior and physical appearance.
