ORIGINAL ANSWER
To clean your computer screen safely, first make sure the screen is powered off as it's easier to see dust and smudges on a dark screen. Use a clean and dry microfiber cloth, which is safe for your screens, to lightly wipe the screen. Avoid touching the LCD screen as it can shorten the lifespan. If there are stubborn spots, do not rub harder as this could damage the screen. For a deeper clean, you can make a solution of 1 part isopropyl alcohol and 1 part distilled water, spray it on a lint-free cloth, and use it to wipe the screen. Always clean in one direction to prevent streaks. Never use window or abrasive cleaners as they can damage and yellow the screen. Clean the edges of your LCD screen with a clean cotton ball or swab.
