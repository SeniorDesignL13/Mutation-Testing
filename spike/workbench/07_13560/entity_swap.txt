To grill pork chops according to allrecipes.com, start by preheating an outdoor grill for medium heat and lightly oil the grate. In a bowl, mix together the seasoned salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, Worcestershire sauce, and smoke flavoring until thoroughly combined. Rinse the pork chops, and sprinkle the wet chops on both sides with the spice mixture. With your hands, massage the spice rub into the meat and allow to stand for 2 minutes. Finally, grill the chops over indirect heat until no longer pink inside, about 12 minutes per side.
