- To bake salmon medium rare in the oven, first preheat your oven to either 180C or 350F.
- Pat the salmon dry with paper towels and season the flesh-side with salt.
- Heat a medium ovenproof nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and add olive oil.
- Place the salmon skin-side down in the skillet and sear until the skin is golden brown and crispy, this should take about 5 minutes.
- Then, turn the salmon and transfer the skillet to your preheated oven.
- Cook the salmon for about 5 minutes, until a cake tester or tip of a paring knife is just warm to the touch when inserted into the middle of the salmon.
