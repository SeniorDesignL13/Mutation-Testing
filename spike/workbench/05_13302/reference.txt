ORIGINAL ANSWER
The average cost of long term care can vary widely. For a 55-year-old with standard health rate, the average cost of long term care insurance is $1,720 per year. For a couple both aged 55, the average is $3,381 per year. In terms of actual care costs in the United States in 2010, it was $205 per day or $6,235 per month for a semi-private room in a nursing home, $229 per day or $6,965 per month for a private room in a nursing home, and $3,293 per month for care in an assisted living facility (for a one-bedroom unit). The cost for a home health aide was $21 per hour.
